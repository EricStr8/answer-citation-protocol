# Changelog

## 3.0.0 — 2026-09-13

- Defines identifier, visible-answer, supporting-content, and structured-data layers.
- Requires material alignment between visible and machine-readable answers.
- Adds accessible interaction requirements.
- Uses established Schema.org vocabulary in the recommended profile.
- Keeps custom properties outside the normative profile pending research.
- Credits Eric Strate as founder and original proposer and EricStrate.com as the original test bed.
- Documents testing as evidence gathering for a possible future Schema.org proposal.

## 2.0.0 — 2026-07-24

- Added JSON-LD answer mapping and provenance experiments.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-07-06

- Published the original proposal and stable answer markers.
